Support / Build the system

Accounting that gives the same numbers, whoever is in charge.

From source documents like invoices and bank statements, we build a system that uses AI to produce the books and the management numbers. Work that depended on one person's experience and judgment moves onto the system, so owners can check where the business stands at any time.

What we do

It isn’t the same as
installing accounting software.

Accounting software is a tool that turns the numbers you enter into books. What we build covers what comes before it and after it — one continuous line from taking in the source documents to the numbers an owner looks at, so the result is the same whoever is in charge.

InTake in

AI reads invoices, receipts and bank statements and creates the journal entries. You can keep receiving paper the way you do now.

MiddleStreamline

Write it on paper, copy it, enter it, tally it again. The chain of copying by hand becomes one line. Paper vouchers and approval stamps are cut back to the ones you actually need.

OutTurn into numbers

Trial balances, monthly figures and cash flow are totaled automatically. Owners can see the business by department, by customer — at whatever level they want.

HandoverMake it stick

Set-up fitted to how your company works, training for the people who use it, and upkeep afterwards — all included.

Before & After

What changes.

Before / Accounting that relies on experience
  • Processing judgment lives in the experience of the staff
  • Categories built for tax filing, hard to use for analysis
  • The numbers come together well after the month ends
  • The same figures copied from paper to software to Excel, again and again
→
After / Accounting run by a system
  • The rules live in the system — same processing whoever takes over
  • Numbers can be rebuilt from the source documents, in the units management needs
  • Each transaction shows up in the numbers as it happens
  • Read once, used to the end — no copying

Time

The time spent copying by hand, back to work only people can do.

Move it onto a system, and that time frees up. The people who have kept the books can spend it reading the numbers, talking with customers and thinking about the next move.

Hiring is hard these days. No longer having to hold up accounting with headcount is also a way of preparing the company to carry on.
