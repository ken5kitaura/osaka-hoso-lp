What changes a system is the people who use it.

However good the system, the company won’t change if the people using it keep working the same way. This is training where your team learns generative AI — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others — by actually using it on their own work.

Why us

The ways we’ve used it
at Radio Osaka ourselves.

At Radio Osaka, a broadcaster we invested in, Osaka Hoso has put generative AI to work on real jobs — from building the accounting system to making programs. The system that produces 2,500–3,000 journal entries a month from source documents, and Daily Radio Osaka, presented by the AI announcer Benten Obi, both came out of that work.

What we teach is what we have tested on our own work.

Curriculum

The programGenerative AI Essentials

In-person plan: 2 days, 11 hours total　／　Online plan: 6 sessions, 11 hours total

Day 1 (5.5 hours)

Morning 3h ／ Lecture
  • The current AI landscape and the characteristics of the Big Three (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
  • Live demo: how AI is used at Radio Osaka — real cases
  • Prompt design fundamentals: how to give AI the right instructions
Afternoon 2.5h ／ Workshop 1
  • Table discussions: "Where can AI make an impact in our business?"
  • Instructor follow-up at each table
  • Idea presentations

Day 2 (5.5 hours)

Morning 3h ／ Deep Dive
  • ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini: task-specific selection strategy
  • Prompt design practice (using your company's actual workflows as material)
  • AI usage guideline drafting workshop
Afternoon 2.5h ／ Workshop 2
  • Create your company's AI adoption plan
  • Finalize the AI usage guideline (draft)
  • Presentations and feedback

Deliverables

What you make during the training.

Your company’s AI plan

The uses you came up with during the training, laid out by department and by priority. When the training ends, you can put it to work in-house right away.

In-house rules for using AI (draft)

Protecting information, copyright, handling data. A draft of the rules for using AI at your company, fitted to how you work.
