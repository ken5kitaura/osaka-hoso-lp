Support

Tasks to machines, work to people.

As the workforce shrinks, a company needs systems that keep it running with fewer people. So we look at daily work and separate routine tasks from real work. Copying from paper, writing the same thing twice, deciding from memory — those go to machines, so people can spend their time on what only people can do.

Accounting is where we start. Accounting isn't only there to pay the right amount of tax. Where is the money made, and where is it going? Only companies whose owners can check that right away, and act on it, will make it through an era that moves this fast.

We move work that depended on people's judgment and effort onto AI and systems, so the same numbers can be rebuilt from the source documents at any time. Then we set it up so owners can see their business at whatever level of detail they need.

The same approach reaches beyond accounting: an HR evaluation system in which AI reads employees' work records and organizes the material for reviews, and workflow analysis that removes copying and double entry. We run both in our own company.

Tax filing stays with your accountant, as before. We take on everything before it.

Case

To keep a broadcaster alive, we started with the numbers.

Keeping Osaka's local broadcaster alive for the next era — that is why Osaka Hoso is involved with Radio Osaka. The first thing we took on was accounting.

Today Radio Osaka produces 2,500–3,000 journal entries a month automatically from source documents, and can see in fine detail where it makes money and where the money goes. Once the numbers were visible, we carried the same approach into HR evaluations and the workflows of sales and programming.
